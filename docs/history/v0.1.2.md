> application-state directory (`/config`) before upgrading.
>
> **Added music is now organized by tags by default.** Uploads, link
> downloads, and online music are placed with the rename pattern unless an
> administrator turns off **Organize added music by tags** in **Admin →
> Settings**. The per-browser switch on the Upload tab is gone, so uploads

> [!NOTE]
> Use `docker compose up -d --pull always` to upgrade. Update `RAINY_IMAGE`
> first if it is pinned to an older release.

Rainy v0.1.2 adds a **Weekly Mix** beside the Daily Mix and puts shelves of
songs on the home page: **Quick picks**, **On repeat**, and **Rediscover**.
**Immersive** now fills the whole window with the cover's colours behind a
see-through interface, and covers colour the app only while it is on. Every
way of adding music follows one server setting for organizing files by tags,
and Rainy offers to fill in tags when added music has no artist or album.

<details>
<summary><strong>Listening</strong></summary>

- **Weekly Mix** joins the Daily Mix under **Made for You**: about 50 songs
  picked every Monday and kept until the next one, on every device. It leans
  towards songs you have never played and artists like the ones you play most,
  and leaves out what you played in the last seven days.
- The home page now has shelves of songs between the album shelves: **Quick
  picks** (songs that suit what you play, with a button for other songs), **On
  repeat** (your most played songs of the last 30 days), and **Rediscover**
  (favorites you haven't heard for a month). Tap a song to play its shelf from
  there.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Look And Feel</strong></summary>

- **Immersive** goes deeper: the cover's colours now fill the whole window as
  soft colour fields, and the sidebar, the header, the page, and cards are
  see-through over them instead of sitting on flat surfaces. From tablets up
  the fields drift slowly while music plays.
- Covers now colour the app only in **Immersive**. With it off, album,
  playlist, and artist pages keep the look of the style in use like every
  other page: no colours taken from the artwork and no blurred cover behind
  the header. The **Album colours** switch is gone from Settings → Appearance;
  turn Immersive on for the cover-coloured look.
- The sidebar is narrower (208 px instead of 240 px), leaving more room for
  the library.
- The home page's greeting no longer gets a bar of its own while you scroll.
  Styles with a header across the top show it there, beside the logo; styles
  that keep everything in the sidebar show nothing.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Library Management</strong></summary>

- **Organize by tags** is now a server setting instead of a switch on the
  Upload tab, and it is on by default: files you pick or drag in, link
  downloads, and online music are all placed with the rename pattern, under the
  folder you chose. Administrators can turn it off with **Organize added music
  by tags** in **Admin → Settings**. The old per-browser switch is gone, so
  uploads that used to keep their names are now organized unless the setting is
  turned off.
- When added music has no artist or album tag, Rainy says so and offers **Fill
  in tags**, which opens the tag editor on those tracks. This covers uploads,
  link downloads, and online music.

</details>
