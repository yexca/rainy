> [!NOTE]
> Use `docker compose up -d --pull always` to upgrade. Update `RAINY_IMAGE`
> first if it is pinned to an older release.

Rainy v0.1.3 makes the player easier to follow, makes playlists portable, and
adds verified application-state backups. It also brings the current Go, web,
and CI dependencies into the release.

<details>
<summary><strong>Playback And The Web Player</strong></summary>

- Now Playing opens on the lyrics, and bilingual lyrics recognize translations
  in brackets such as `待ってよ「等一下」` without mistaking credit lines for
  translations.
- On tablets and desktops, the full-screen player opens the queue beside the
  lyrics while the artwork and controls make room for it.
- Songs that meet the configurable long-song limit (10 minutes by default) are
  left out when a list is played or several songs are queued, and from infinite
  mode suggestions. The song you choose still plays.
- In immersive mode, the next song's colours reveal themselves from the point
  you tapped or clicked, or from its artwork when playback advances on its own.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Library Browsing And Playlists</strong></summary>

- Import UTF-8 M3U/M3U8 playlists with a match preview, library and folder
  selection, and explicit acknowledgement of unmatched entries. Export
  playable songs as M3U8 while preserving order and duplicates.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Library Management</strong></summary>

- Sticky selection bars on Tracks, the trash view, and history headers now use
  the immersive glass treatment so cover colours remain visible behind them.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Operations And Deployment</strong></summary>

- Create database-and-key snapshots with `rainy backup create <name>` and
  verify their hashes, SQLite integrity, migration compatibility, and stored
  credentials with `rainy backup verify <name>` before restoring them.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Development And Docs</strong></summary>

- Refresh the Go, web, and GitHub Actions dependencies, including patched
  toolchain and frontend packages used by the release build.

</details>
