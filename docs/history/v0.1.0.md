> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Back up your application-state directory (`/config` in the current image).**
> This is Rainy's first release. The state folder holds the
> database and `secret.key`, which encrypts stored passwords; back up both
> together before every upgrade. Future releases migrate the database
> automatically on startup. If managers will edit tags or rename files, back up
> your music as well.
>
> **The default mounts have changed in this re-publication.** Application state
> now uses `./config:/config` and music uses `./data:/data`, replacing the old
> `/data` (state) and `/music` (music) container paths. Preserve the existing
> database and `secret.key`, update the mounts, and edit the existing library
> path instead of deleting and recreating it. Follow
> [Upgrading the mount layout](../operations/docker.md#upgrading-the-mount-layout)
> before updating an existing installation; explicit environment overrides can
> keep the legacy paths.

> [!NOTE]
> Install with the production `docker-compose.yml` and a `.env` file, then run
> `docker compose up -d --pull always`. Set `RAINY_IMAGE` to pin this release
> instead of following `latest`.

Rainy v0.1.0 is the first public release: a self-hosted music server for NAS
devices with a Subsonic API, an installable web player, and library management
built in.

## Playback And The Web Player

- Stream MP3, FLAC, AAC/M4A/ALAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus, WAV, AIFF, APE, WavPack,
  WMA, DSD, and more, with seeking on original files and on-the-fly
  transcoding to MP3, Opus, or AAC through ffmpeg.
- Play from an iOS-style interface: a floating mini player on phones, a
  full-screen Now Playing sheet with artwork-colored backgrounds, synced
  lyrics, a draggable queue, shuffle and repeat, and a desktop player bar with
  side panels.
- On tablets and desktops, switch between the bottom bar, a floating Now
  Playing window, and a floating mini bar in the bottom-right corner; full
  screen stays available from all of them. The bottom bar can auto-hide,
  leaving a small handle that shows it on hover and pins it on click, and
  dragging sideways on the mini bar seeks.
- Read bilingual lyrics with the translation in smaller text under each
  original line, both for lines that share a timestamp and for
  `original 中文` lines, and hide or show translations with a button beside
  the lyrics button or a setting under Playback. Japanese and Korean lines use
  matching Japanese or Korean fonts.
- Open **picture-in-picture lyrics**: a small always-on-top window with the
  line being sung, its translation, the next line, and playback controls (a
  mini player in Chrome and Edge, a lyrics video in Safari on Mac, iPhone, and
  iPad).
- Keep the music going with **infinite mode** (∞ in Playing Next, or Settings
  → Playback): when two songs or fewer are left, similar songs from your
  library are added to the queue, marked with ∞; songs you add yourself play
  before them, and it waits while repeat is on.
- Control playback from lock screens, headsets, and media keys through Media
  Session, use AirPlay on iOS, and use keyboard shortcuts on desktop.
- Choose streaming quality, conversion format, ReplayGain normalization, and
  next-song preloading per device.
- Install the web app as a PWA on iOS, Android, and desktop.
- Resume the play queue across the web app and Subsonic clients.
- Listen to internet radio stations added by an administrator.

## Listening And Scrobbling

- See a personal **Listening** report (Library → Listening on phones) for the
  last 7, 30, or 90 days, one of the last 12 calendar months, the last 12
  months, all time, or a calendar year: plays and listening time compared with
  the period before, new artists and songs, days with music and the longest
  streak, plays over time, a weekday-by-hour chart of when you listen, top
  artists, albums, songs, and genres, and the players you used. A **History**
  tab lists every play by day, and songs removed from the library stay in the
  history under their old names.
- Open month and year reports with a **recap**: listening time, top artist and
  song, new artists and songs, when you listen most, the longest streak, and
  the change against the month or year before. In the first week of a month
  the home page links to last month's recap.
- Get a **Daily Mix** under **Made for You** on the home page: about 30 songs
  picked each day from your library (artists similar to your favorites,
  favorites you haven't heard in a month, never played songs from your top
  genres) that stay the same all day on every device. Picks are computed on
  the server from your own plays, stars, and ratings only.
- **Scrobble** to Last.fm or ListenBrainz (off by default): after an
  administrator turns it on in Admin → Scrobbling (Last.fm also needs an API
  account there), users connect their accounts in Settings → Scrobbling. Plays
  from the web app and Subsonic apps, "now playing", and (for Last.fm) starred
  songs as loves are sent to the service; plays wait on the server and are
  retried while the service is unreachable.

## Look And Feel

- Meet Rainy's mascot. She is the app icon and appears on empty library pages,
  "no results", error, 404, and no-access screens, the startup screen, the
  listening report before your first play, an empty trash, a clean doctor
  report, the library scan panel while a scan runs, beside the sign-in card on
  wide screens, and in Settings. On tablets and desktops she sits in the
  bottom-right corner above the player, listens along while music plays, and
  dozes off after a while; click her for a short line or hide her with ×.
  **Settings → Mascot** turns the illustrations and the companion off
  separately.
- Album, playlist, and artist pages take on the colours of their artwork, like
  Apple Music: the page background follows the cover's main colour (a deep
  tone in the dark theme or for dark covers, a pale tint otherwise) and
  buttons and links use its most vivid colour, with text kept readable. The
  sidebar, player, and menus keep your theme. Turn **Album colours** off in
  Settings → Appearance to keep the plain theme.
- On tablets and desktops an app header runs across the top, with the sidebar
  toggle and logo on the left and the account menu on the right.

## Library Browsing And Search

- Browse home, albums, artists, songs, genres, favorites, and playlists, with
  pinyin indexing for Chinese artist names and article-aware sorting.
- Search across artists, albums, and songs regardless of case, accents, and
  full-width characters.
- Star and rate tracks, albums, and artists; build personal and public
  playlists.
- Scan on startup, on a schedule, or on demand; detect moved files without
  losing favorites or play counts; and skip hidden, NAS system, and
  `.rainyignore` folders.
- Repair GBK, Big5, and Shift-JIS mojibake when reading tags, without
  modifying files.
- Read the valid ID3 metadata in WAV files even when legacy-encoded LIST/INFO
  tags are also present, without modifying the original audio file.

## Library Management

- Find everything under three sidebar entries (tabs on phones): **Tracks**
  (Metadata, Upload, Links, and Online, sharing one destination folder),
  **Library tools** (Folders, Doctor, Trash, History), and **Admin** (Users,
  Libraries, and the server settings pages Settings, yt-dlp, Sources,
  Scrobbling, and System).
- Edit tags for one track or many, with "multiple values" handling, raw tag
  access, and preview-before-apply tools: auto-number, tags from file name,
  find and replace, change case, copy field, and clear field.
- Search NetEase Cloud Music, QQ Music, Kugou, Kuwo, and iTunes from the tag
  editor for tags, covers, and lyrics (with optional paired translations),
  once an administrator enables online metadata lookup. A chosen result only
  fills the editor; nothing is written until you save. With several tracks
  selected, only album-level fields are offered.
- Embed covers or save folder images, and edit embedded or `.lrc` lyrics with
  a line-stamping helper that keeps a paired translation on the same time. The
  lyrics editor recognizes bilingual lyrics written as `original 中文`,
  previews the split, and on request rewrites timed lines as standard
  same-timestamp bilingual LRC.
- Rename and organize files by pattern with a conflict-aware preview.
- Upload files and folders, optionally organized by tags.
- Download the audio of YouTube and bilibili videos with yt-dlp in Tracks →
  Links (off by default): paste a link (bilibili share text works too), pick
  the original audio or M4A, MP3, or Opus, and optionally the whole playlist
  (up to 100 entries). Files land in the chosen folder or follow "Organize by
  tags", get title, artist, date, the source link, and a square cover written
  through TagLib, and appear in the edit history. Downloads keep running when
  you leave the page.
- Search Kuwo, Kugou, QQ Music, NetEase Cloud Music, and Migu in Tracks →
  Online and download songs into the library in the quality you choose (off by
  default), with the catalogue's tags, lyrics (translation paired), and cover
  written through TagLib. Links come from lx-music custom source scripts that
  an administrator imports; Rainy includes none. Several sources can be tried
  in priority order automatically, or one fixed source used; like lx-music, an
  expired link is requested from the same source once more before moving on.
  Downloads appear in the edit history with the song's page, quality, and
  source.
- Delete to a restorable trash, find problems with the library doctor, rewrite
  garbled tags as UTF-8, browse folders, and review every change in the edit
  history.

## Subsonic Clients

- Implement the Subsonic API 1.16.1 with the OpenSubsonic `formPost`,
  `songLyrics`, `transcodeOffset`, `apiKeyAuthentication`, and
  `indexBasedQueue` extensions.
- Support password, token, and API-key authentication, with per-user API keys
  managed in Settings.
- Support full-library sync through empty `search3` queries for clients such
  as Symfonium.

## Accounts And Security

- Create the first administrator on first visit; manage users with
  administrator, manager, and download permissions.
- Use HttpOnly session cookies with sliding expiry, rate-limited sign-in, and
  hashed session tokens and API keys.
- Confine every file operation to the library roots and record it in the edit
  history.

## Operations And Deployment

- Store the database, encryption key, caches, trash, uploads, and yt-dlp under
  `/config` by default, with user music mounted at `/data`. The entrypoint fixes
  ownership only in the application-state directory. Path environment variables
  remain available for custom and legacy mounts.
- Ship one Docker image for `linux/amd64` and `linux/arm64` on Docker Hub
  (`yexca/rainy`) and the GitHub Container Registry. The image includes
  QuickJS (about 2 MiB), the JavaScript runtime yt-dlp needs for YouTube;
  yt-dlp itself is not bundled.
- Configure the production Compose stack through `.env`: image, music and data
  paths, host port, `PUID`/`PGID`/`UMASK`, time zone, scan schedule, log level,
  proxy trust, and session lifetime.
- Run as an unprivileged user after fixing data-folder ownership, with clear
  startup messages for permission problems and read-only music mounts.
- Reset passwords and list users with the `rainy user` command.
- Keep every outbound feature off until an administrator turns it on:
  - **Online metadata lookup** contacts the catalogues above when a manager
    searches; a second setting, **Pretend to search from mainland China**,
    adds a spoofed mainland-China `X-Real-IP` header to the Chinese
    catalogues' requests. See
    [Privacy](../../PRIVACY.md#online-metadata-lookup).
  - The **yt-dlp** tab in Admin allows downloads from YouTube and bilibili,
    checks for and installs yt-dlp updates (the official GitHub release,
    verified against its checksums, installed into `/config/ytdlp`), and takes
    optional sign-in cookies. Cookies are account credentials: Rainy shows a
    warning first, keeps only the site's own cookies, stores them encrypted
    with `secret.key`, and never returns them. `RAINY_YTDLP_PATH` points Rainy
    at a yt-dlp you manage yourself instead. See
    [Privacy](../../PRIVACY.md#downloads-from-youtube-and-bilibili).
  - The **Sources** tab in Admin allows online music and music sources:
    import lx-music source scripts from a file or a link, order them, enable
    or disable each, test them, update them from their link, and see their
    update notices; choose automatic fallback or one fixed source. Scripts run
    on the server in a sandbox that allows public internet addresses only. See
    [Privacy](../../PRIVACY.md#online-music-and-music-sources).
